About

I received my B.Sc. in Computer Engineering from Amirkabir University of Technology. During my undergrad, I was a research assistant at the Robust & Interpretable Machine Learning (RIML) Lab at Sharif University of Technology, working with Prof. Mohammad Hossein Rohban. I also worked as a Software Engineer at Divar.ir.

My interests lie in reinforcement learning, neural network plasticity and sparsity, continual learning, representation learning.
